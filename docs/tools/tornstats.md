[TornStats](https://tornstats.com/){:target="_blank"} is an essential tool offering detailed statistics and insights into your Torn gameplay. As a faction member, you will share information such as battle stats, natural nerve bar, CPR, and other relevant data.

!!! note "Torn Stats operates independently"
    Torn Stats is a separate service that uses your Torn API key and Torn ID.
    It does **not** interact with your Torn game account beyond reading this data.
    The site is **unobtrusive**, and the email field is only used in case you forget your password.
    Your gameplay and account remain unaffected.

If you haven't signed up already, follow these steps:

## 1. Sign up

1. Go to the [TornStats](https://tornstats.com/){:target="_blank"} website.
2. Click on **Sign up!**
![TornStats login page](../assets/images/tools/tornstats/sign_up.png)
3. Fill out all required fields, including:
    - Username (Make sure it's the same as the torn ID)
    - Your **Torn ID**
    - A **Limited Access API key**
![TornStats sign up page](../assets/images/tools/tornstats/important_fields.png)

## 2. Apply to the faction

Once your account is created, apply to the faction using the link below:

- [Apply to the faction on TornStats](https://www.tornstats.com/factions/show/6576/apply){:target="_blank"}

After applying, go to **User Settings** on TornStats and make sure **Share Stats** is set to either:

- **With Faction Members**, or
- **With Leaders Only**

## 3. Share CPR
1. Go to your **TornStats settings**.
2. Ensure your **CPR (Checkpoint Pass Rate)** is enabled.
3. If your CPR is disabled, update your sharing settings so faction leadership can view it.
![Share CPR with faction](https://i.postimg.cc/sXDs9JyR/CPR-enable.gif)
