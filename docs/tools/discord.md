Discord is the primary platform used across **7 Deadly Sins\*** for coordination, communication, and automation. It is required for organised crimes, wars, chains, and faction administration, including banking. The steps below walk you through the required setup.



## Join Discord Servers

1. Join **Torn’s Official Discord** server:
   [Torn Official Discord](https://discord.gg/TVstvww){:target="_blank"}
2. Join the **7 Deadly Sins\*** Discord server:
   [7 Deadly Sins\* Discord](https://discord.gg/3Y2PBSBnJk){:target="_blank"}




## Torn Discord Verification

1. Upon joining Torn’s Discord server for the first time, the Torn bot will send you a direct message (DM).
<br>
![Torn bot](../assets/images/tools/discord/torn_avatar.png)

2. In the DM conversation, type `!verify` and send it.
<br>
![Torn bot message](../assets/images/tools/discord/torn_new_message.png)

3. Click the verification link sent by the Torn bot.
4. You will be redirected to a page with a verification button. Click it to confirm your Torn nickname.
<br>
![Button page from torn](../assets/images/tools/discord/torn_click_to_verify_button.png)

5. Check your Torn logs to confirm the verification was successful.
<br>
![Highlighted Logs button](../assets/images/tools/discord/torn_highlight_logs.png)
![Display Open logs](../assets/images/tools/discord/torn_check_logs_discord_verification.png)

## Verify on the 7 Deadly Sins\* Discord

1. Return to the **7 Deadly Sins\*** Discord server.
2. In the designated verification channel, type `/verify` and press Enter.
    - Ensure the command is handled by the **Tornium bot**, not another bot.
<br>
![Server verification command](../assets/images/tools/discord/tornium_verification_server.png)
